How it works

1. Create your account — register with your name, email, and school path. Your profile visibility is yours to control.

2. Find your school — search 588 institutions across Federal, State, and Private ownership. Open any school to see its faculties, departments, programmes, and campus info.

3. Connect with students — discover fellow prospective and existing students, send connection requests, and build your circle before resumption.

4. Join the community — ask questions, share guidance, and meet mentors in your school community. Unsafe behavior can be reported on any post.

About Fidonect

Mission: make every Nigerian student feel connected before arrival, supported while there, and connected to opportunities after graduation.

Who it serves: prospective students (UTME/DE, newly admitted, awaiting resumption), existing students and mentors, plus alumni — across universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education.

What you get: verified institution directory, course and campus guidance, classmate discovery, mentorship connections, and community discussions.

Free core, always: registration, discovery, connection, and community participation stay free. Trust, safety, and usefulness come before monetization.

Safety & privacy

Your safety: block and report users or content that bothers you; harassment, scams, and spam are reviewed by moderators. Never share passwords, bank details, ID documents, or your exact home address.

Your privacy: you control profile visibility (everyone, connections, or only you). Phone numbers and exact addresses are never public by default. We collect only what matching needs, and never sell personal information.

AI honesty: guidance is labeled Official, Community, or AI — always verify fees, deadlines, and admission facts with official school sources.
